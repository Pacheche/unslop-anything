# Photography-led editorial direction

Use this direction for marketing and editorial sites when the brief calls for it. Start from the brand, message and photography available to the project. For a redesign, inventory the sections, routes, forms, states, accessibility, analytics and metadata before editing. Check the affected journeys against that inventory after the visual work.

- Choose the hero around the lead asset. Use a full-bleed photograph when its crop leaves room for copy. Use a split, framed figure or text-led composition when the image needs context, detail, or a different crop on mobile. Decide the arrangement from the content of this page.
- Use scale, whitespace and a restrained type hierarchy to make the page scannable. Derive the grid and color ramp from project tokens. Monochrome, a 5 px rhythm, Geist and glass are options for a brief that supports them.
- Give each photo a role and check its license. Art-direct the narrow crop and check text contrast against the actual image. Use hairline diagrams when they explain a mechanism. Decoration can stay quiet.
- Let a single entrance establish the hierarchy. Content and links remain available before JavaScript loads and with reduced motion. Keep native scrolling unless the experience actually requires a different behavior. Check small and wide layouts, reload with motion reduced, and exercise keyboard and form states.
